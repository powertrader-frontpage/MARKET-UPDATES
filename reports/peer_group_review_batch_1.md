- Banking remains a parent business family, but private, public and small finance banks stay in separate peer groups for relative-strength ranking.

- Pharmaceutical formulations remain separate from API/CDMO and biotech-led businesses.
- Hospitals and diagnostics remain separate; their operating economics differ.
- Cement manufacturers remain a dedicated peer group.
- No live dashboard membership, score, breadth, zone, weight or ranking is changed by this review.
