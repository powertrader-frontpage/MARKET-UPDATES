## Key separation rules

- Hotels, online travel platforms and airlines are separate peer groups.
- Residential developers should not automatically be combined with mall owners or mixed-use property operators.
- Mobile telecom operators remain separate from tower infrastructure and enterprise connectivity.
- Broadcasters, print media, cinema exhibition, music IP and gaming are distinct media models.
- Housing finance, affordable housing finance, asset management, insurance, wealth management, gold loans and project finance remain separate financial peer groups.
- PAYTM and GROWW should not be treated as direct peers merely because both are digital financial platforms.
